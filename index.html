About

Twenty years of work where every word matters.

I have worked since 2006 in roles where small mistakes have real costs. For eight years I was an IT and network engineer, running networks for a major telecom company and a medical corporation and writing incident reports. At the telecom company I also reviewed lease and land-right contracts for new base stations, checking both the Japanese wording and the terms. Since 2021 I have worked at a Japanese government agency, handling payroll, employee benefits, information security and digital transformation. Now I bring that same care to Japanese language quality for AI and global teams.
